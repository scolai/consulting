Process

Onboarding

Free Consultation

Typically up to one half hour. Tell me about your project and we discover if we are a good match. We discuss such things as

  • Goals
  • Timelines
  • Constraints
  • Technological Preferences (if any)
  • Budget

Quote

I provide you with a pricing structure. This can be fixed or ranges, depending on our initial discussion.

Statement of Work

The statement of work itemizes my view of the project development, based on information gathered, including timelines and quote.

SOW Review & Client Approval

Upon your review we can go over any details, make adjustments and amendments. The point here is to have a clear picture what moving forward on the project entails.

Contract & Agreement

The SOW will be provided alongside the contract.

Initial Payment

Typically the fees are rendered into two payments. The initial payment (50% of quote) is required for work to begin.

Note: The initial payment is non-refundable.

Design Phase

First Deliverable - Initial Design & Prototyping

Cycle of Revisions

  • Typically up to two or three rounds of revision
  • Agreed upon definitions of what is revisable and what is not
  • Each revision round ends with a client sign-off

Design Final Approval

  • You sign-off on final approval
  • Your sign-off confirms that all design decisions, content development, layout, structure and any other details pertaining to the statement of work are final.
  • The sign-off confirms that changes after this point may incur additional cost.

Development & Final Sign-off

Build proceeds according to the statement of work and the finalized design.

Client Review

You review and confirm the final product and functionality.

Note: This is *not* a review for further revisions. As agreed, additional revisions at this point may incur additional costs.

Final Payment & Release

Final Balance Payment

Final payment is required and receipt is confirmed before product release.

Release of Product

Client receives product in full. This typically includes

  • Deployment
  • Deployment Package
  • Documentation
  • Credentials transfer
  • Training Session

Support Phase

Limited Post-launch Support

The limits of the post-launch support phase is typically agreed upon in the statement of work, and may include

  • Duration (typically between one to four weeks)
  • What troubleshooting includes (bugs, breakages, clarifications).
  • What troubleshooting does not include
    • New features or design/content/layout changes
    • hosting issues
    • incompatibilities with 3rd-parties
    • Any issues not directly caused by the product
  • Credentials transfer
  • Training Session
